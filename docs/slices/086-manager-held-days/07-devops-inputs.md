**Red flags for the brief (not mine to decide):**
1. **Scope conflict.** The founder says "all of their reporting employees". Her rule of 17 Oct is that acting stops at **direct** reports. Viewing the whole line is fine; bulk filing for skip-level staff would reverse that rule. Owner: `hrms-product-manager` / Surbhi. Default: list the whole line, file for direct reports only.
2. **One missing time for many days.** A held request carries a missing time and which punch is missing, per day. A bulk action that applies one time to many days writes guessed data into pay. Owner: UX and PM. The shape of the screen decides this.
3. **Pay effect at scale.** A manager's click only creates requests; HR's approval changes pay. Keep that split, or the 085 rule "nobody decides a request they raised" is lost.
