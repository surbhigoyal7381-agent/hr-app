{#- ONE place that turns a tenant's brand colour into the palette every Alvoraa surface uses: the login page, the employee portal, the driver and vendor portals, and the field check-in app on a phone. It exists because there were three implementations and they disagreed. The design system clamped lightness in HSL; the login page had its own shadeHex() and deepen() and fetched the colour by API *after* first paint; the field app briefly had a third, in Python. One jeweller therefore saw three different shades of their own purple, and a flash of the wrong one on the way in. Include this ONCE per page, in , before any stylesheet that uses the tokens. It runs before first paint, so nothing repaints. Needs `primary_color` and `accent_color` in the page context - which means the page's get_context() must call alvoraa_portal.tenant_context.get_branding(). A page that does not is not broken: the script returns early and the built-in palette stands. The lightness clamp is the part that matters and the part not to soften. A tenant may choose a pale yellow as their brand; they may not make their staff's buttons unreadable. This is the only place that can be enforced for every surface at once. -#}
