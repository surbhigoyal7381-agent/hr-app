Option B (recommended): the button lives on the form itself

In CRM the Sales Manager presses Open beside the form's link (already there). The form opens with this box at the top, only when signed in as a Sales Manager or System Manager — the same rule CRM already uses for its draft preview.

Why: it changes no CRM screen, so nothing to carry through CRM upgrades. It also makes the organiser look at the real form before printing (“test from your phone”).

If the form is not published, the box says instead: “This form is a draft. Publish it in CRM before printing a QR — a QR for a draft opens a page attendees cannot use.” and has no button.

Why the rating reads “1 · Very poor”. CRM sorts a choice field by its text. A number at the front makes “lowest first” a plain ascending sort. Without it, “Good” would sort before “OK”.

Why “Rating is set”. Blank ratings sort to the top in an ascending sort, so the people who did not rate would push the unhappy ones off the first screen. Entries with no rating are counted in the second view.

“News OK” is the only column that says who may get marketing (conflict C3). A blank means: do not email them about other events.
