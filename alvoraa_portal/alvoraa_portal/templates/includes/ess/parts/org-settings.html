Performance reviews
A review takes its own copy of each objective and KPI, so the numbers it was decided on stay put. These three settings say when those numbers close, how long the originals stay locked, and what happens to an item taken out of a review.
After this point, later progress and evidence no longer change the review.
While a review holds an objective or KPI, its name, target, weight and period cannot change. Use 0 to keep it locked until the review is finished.
An item that already has a rating is always kept and marked removed, whichever setting is chosen.
Key Result Areas
KRAs are the areas of responsibility that belong to a role. They come from the employee's appraisal template, so every objective can be traced back to what the job is for.
When on, an objective cannot be activated without a KRA. Drafts are still allowed, so nobody is blocked while HR assigns an appraisal template.
