Why we are asking

Your HR system is built for your organisation alone — your own address, your own staff, your own leave rules. Nothing is shared with anyone else. To build it we need the facts below. Most of it you already have; it takes about thirty minutes to fill in.

Sections A to D are needed to create your system. The rest can follow within a week, but the sooner they arrive, the sooner your people can use it.
