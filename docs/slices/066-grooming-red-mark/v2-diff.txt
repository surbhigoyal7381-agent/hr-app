DIFFERENCES THAT NEED SURBHI'S YES
Y1  When the flag rises at the legal limit. The manager never gets a button once the limit is
    reached (spec AC-47), so no mark is ever "tried and stopped". v2 raises the flag at the
    mark that REACHES the limit (Rahul, Senior on Rs 18,000: at his 2nd mark). The other way:
    keep the button, refuse on the server, and raise the flag from the refused try - this
    needs the refused try to be stored. Recommend the drawn way (nothing new stored).
Y2  Where the review note lives. Spec 7.6 / AC-62 keeps it as an ordinary manager note (an
    Info comment, shown in the Employee form timeline and HR's Manager Notes list). v2 draws
    it private, per the request: HR in scope and the direct manager read it; the employee sees
    only "Action agreed on {date}"; it cannot be changed or deleted. Spec and security (06)
    must follow. A marker who is not the direct manager sees the flag but not the note.
Y3  HR's tick reads "I have told Rahul in person" (no "today"), because HR marks past days.
    Spec R13 is manager-only today and must cover HR.
Y4  The "Grooming review pending" list is not configuration: the flag is worked out, not
    stored, so Report Builder cannot filter on it. It needs a small script report or a stored
    field (analyst and engineer). "People with grooming reds this month" is a saved view.
    The two v1 views stay; v2 adds these two (four in all). Say if they should replace any.
Y5  Not drawn, words only: when the limit raises the flag, the employee's bell has no line
    for it (spec N1 covers only the 4th mark). Proposed: "You have reached this month's legal
    limit for grooming reds, so HR will talk to you about it." HR's N6 variant: "Grooming
    review pending for Rahul Sharma: the legal limit for grooming reds in October is reached."
Y6  The note guidance names religion as well as health and family: "Keep it to dress and the
    action you agreed. Do not write about health, family, religion or anything else personal."
Y7  The 4th-mark employee state imagines Rahul on Basic Rs 30,000 (Sample) - a Senior needs at
    least Rs 26,667 to reach a 4th mark. v1's "Legal limit reached" employee state (5 marks)
    is left as approved; parity D-3 in the spec still applies to it.
