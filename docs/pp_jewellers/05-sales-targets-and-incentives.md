# 05 — Sales targets and incentives

Decision from the review: **no incentive calculation engine**. Incentives are salary components (file 04) that HR enters as Additional Salary each month from the store's sales report. Targets and attainment are shown separately as KPIs in the goals module (file 09), so the demo shows both: "here is what the person achieved" and "here is what it paid". The figures below are illustrative demo numbers, not the client's real scheme.

## 1. Article categories

| Category | Share of store sales (demo) | Why it has its own incentive |
|---|---|---|
| Gold | 60% | volume business, thin margin |
| Diamond | 25% | high margin, certification matters |
| Silver & Fashion | 8% | volume, units |
| Platinum & Gemstone | 7% | specialist selling |

## 3. How a target reaches a person

| Level | Target rule |
|---|---|
| Store | from the table above |
| Floor | store × category share (Gold 60, Diamond 25, Silver & Fashion 8; Platinum & Gemstone is sold from the Diamond floor and adds its 7% there) |
| Individual | floor target split by shares: Senior Sales Executive 1.5, Sales Executive 1.0, Trainee 0.5. A Gold floor has 4 × 1.5 + 8 × 1.0 + 1 × 0.5 = 14.5 shares. Monthly target = quarterly ÷ 3. |

Example, Chandigarh Gold floor, Q2: 12.0 × 60% = 7.2 crore → one share = 7.2 / 14.5 = 49.7 lakh → Senior Sales Executive target = 74.5 lakh for the quarter, about 24.8 lakh per month.

## 4. Incentive slabs (illustrative, entered by hand)

| Who | Basis | Below 90% | 90 to 99% | 100 to 119% | 120% and above |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Senior Sales Executive, Sales Executive, Trainee | own sales in the category | 0 | 0.20% (Gold), 0.60% (Diamond), 0.30% (Silver), 0.50% (Platinum) | 0.35% / 0.90% / 0.50% / 0.80% | 0.50% / 1.20% / 0.70% / 1.00% |
| Floor Manager | floor sales | 0 | 0.10% | 0.15% | 0.20% |
| Assistant Store Manager | store sales | 0 | 0.05% | 0.08% | 0.10% |
| Store In-charge | store sales | 0 | 0.05% | 0.08% | 0.10% |
| Cashier, Customer Relationship Executive | store attainment | 0 | ₹1,500 | ₹2,500 | ₹4,000 (Store Performance Incentive) |

Everyone else (valuers, karigars, vault, security, housekeeping, head office) has no sales incentive. A Festival Working Allowance of ₹500 per holiday worked applies to all store staff and is entered by HR.

## 5. Entry process (what HR does each month)

1. Store Accountant sends the category-wise sales per salesperson for the previous month (in real life from the POS; for the demo, `data/sales_actuals_july.csv`, generated by `demo/pp_jewellers/generate_sales_actuals.py`: 190 salespeople, 150 of them earning an incentive).
2. HR opens **Employee Incentive** (or Additional Salary directly) per employee with the right component and amount, `payroll_date` in the current month. Employee Incentive creates the Additional Salary on submit.
3. Payroll Entry for the month picks the lines up automatically.

For the demo, create Employee Incentive records for all 150 salespeople with an incentive in `sales_actuals_july.csv` (a loop in `bench console`, file 11), plus 15 Floor Managers and 5 Store In-charges at their store's July attainment, plus Store Performance Incentive for the 35 Cashiers and CREs in the four stores at or above 90%. PPJ-0054 gets Gold Incentive ₹9,200 (actual 26.3 lakh against 24.8 lakh target, 106%, slab 0.35%).

## 6. Where the same numbers appear as KPIs

The July actuals are also posted as **KPI Progress Log** entries on each person's "Own sales value vs target" KPI (file 09), so the Store In-charge sees attainment per person and the employee sees their own progress bar. This is the "goals simulation" the review asked for. The two are entered separately for the demo; the link between them is a future automation (see `KPI_AUTOMATION_STRATEGY.md`, domain "Sales Invoice → revenue", which is unbuilt).
